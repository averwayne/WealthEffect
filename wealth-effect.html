The evidence

Is owning assets the way to close the wealth gap?

Yes, with one correction. Here's what the research says, and how each finding shaped this assistant.

The theory holds. Wealth in America sits in owned assets: stocks, retirement accounts, homes and businesses. The top 10% hold about 87% of stocks, and homeowners' median net worth is roughly 38 times renters'. Returns also rise with wealth, so owners pull further ahead.

The correction: knowledge isn't the main barrier. Classes alone barely change behavior and fade fast. What moves people is the on-ramp: a cash cushion, an account to invest in, automatic contributions, low fees, and staying invested through drops. So this is built as an assistant that walks you through those steps, with lessons arriving just in time.
